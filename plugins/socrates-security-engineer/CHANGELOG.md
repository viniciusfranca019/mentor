## [0.3.0] - 2026-11-02

### Added

- The `security-engineer` course becomes its own plugin, which requires `socrates`. It
  starts at 0.3.0 because the content is the same as version 0.2.0 of the Mentor plugin,
  where it was born: a track of 42 modules in 8 phases, axes and diagnosis, and modules
  00 and 01.
- `curso` skill: the bridge that tells the engine where the content is (course contract,
  version 1).
